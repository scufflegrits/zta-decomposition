# ADR-006 — Approved management attachments

**Decision ID:** ADR-006  
**Decision status:** Required  
**Source:** [Handoff section 5](../../zta-decomposition-handoff.md#5-established-architectural-decisions)

## Decision

Use an approved physical management switchport machine or an approved VM with exactly one management vNIC.

## Rationale

Constrain management entry points.

## Consequences

Validate host bridging, forwarding, NAT, virtual-switch permissions and host management controls.

## Alternatives and open selection

Secondary management VM network interfaces and unauthorized bypasses are excluded.

This record preserves the handoff baseline. Rationale and consequences are editorial explanations for review; they do not represent an additional approval or completed engineering test.
