# ADR-004 — Selective inter-VLAN routing

**Decision ID:** ADR-004  
**Decision status:** Required  
**Source:** [Handoff section 5](../../zta-decomposition-handoff.md#5-established-architectural-decisions)

## Decision

ISR routers handle selected authorized inter-VLAN communications.

## Rationale

Support necessary flows with explicit reachability policy.

## Consequences

Define the VLAN allow matrix and ACLs; never add management routing.

## Alternatives and open selection

Unrestricted inter-VLAN routing is excluded; exact allowed flows are undecided.

This record preserves the handoff baseline. Rationale and consequences are editorial explanations for review; they do not represent an additional approval or completed engineering test.
