# ADR-013 — Modular documentation

**Decision ID:** ADR-013  
**Decision status:** Required  
**Source:** [Handoff section 5](../../zta-decomposition-handoff.md#5-established-architectural-decisions)

## Decision

Maintain independently owned Markdown documents and reviewed version-control changes.

## Rationale

Support engineering ownership and maintainable docs-as-code.

## Consequences

Use relative links and the auditor. Git initialization and an initial commit/push are complete; engineering ownership acceptance and a documented review workflow remain pending.

## Alternatives and open selection

A single large report is excluded as the authoritative maintenance structure.

This record preserves the handoff baseline. Rationale and consequences are editorial explanations for review; they do not represent an additional approval or completed engineering test.
