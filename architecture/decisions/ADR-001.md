# ADR-001 — Physical-site-only user authentication

<!-- zta-doc: id=DOC-ADR-001; parent=ROOT; lineage=ROOT -->

**Owner (proposed):** Architecture / relevant pillar owners  
**Document status:** Proposed reference architecture; implementation unvalidated  
**Baseline:** October 24, 2026

**Decision ID:** ADR-001  
**Decision status:** Required  
**Source:** [Handoff section 5](../../zta-decomposition-handoff.md#5-established-architectural-decisions)

## Decision

Users must be physically present at Site A or Site B to initiate authorized user authentication.

## Rationale

Limit authentication initiation to approved physical sites.

## Consequences

Document and test presence enforcement without assuming that a VLAN proves physical presence.

## Alternatives and open selection

Off-site user initiation is excluded; the specific enforcement method remains undecided.

This record preserves the handoff baseline. Rationale and consequences are editorial explanations for review; they do not represent an additional approval or completed engineering test.
