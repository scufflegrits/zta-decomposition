# ADR-009 — Public WAN transport protection

<!-- zta-doc: id=DOC-ADR-009; parent=ROOT; lineage=ROOT -->

**Owner (proposed):** Architecture / relevant pillar owners  
**Document status:** Proposed reference architecture; implementation unvalidated  
**Baseline:** October 24, 2026

**Decision ID:** ADR-009  
**Decision status:** Proposed  
**Source:** [Handoff section 5](../../zta-decomposition-handoff.md#5-established-architectural-decisions)

## Decision

Require approved WAN protection; Palo Alto IPsec is the current candidate.

## Rationale

Protect authorized inter-site communications over the public WAN.

## Consequences

Select the approved profile, peers, routing and key lifecycle; validate behavior before deployment claims.

## Alternatives and open selection

Other approved mechanisms remain possible; selection is unfinished.

This record preserves the handoff baseline. Rationale and consequences are editorial explanations for review; they do not represent an additional approval or completed engineering test.
