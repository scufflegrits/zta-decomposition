# ADR-014 — Operationally bounded ZTA and documented customer acceptance

**Decision ID:** ADR-014  
**Decision status:** Required project direction; contractual adoption and individual customer acceptance remain to be recorded  
**Source:** User direction during project planning on October 25, 2026: implement useful ZTA protections without reducing functionality or imposing undue process; document limitations for customer disposition.

## Decision

Implement ZTA protections to the practical extent supported by equipment and customer-approved operational constraints. Record implemented protections, verified behavior, limitations, functional/workflow impacts, residual risk and compensating controls or their absence. Submit the documented outcome for customer disposition against the applicable contract SOW requirements. Do not use a coverage percentage as an acceptance criterion.

This refines the universal MACsec objective in [ADR-008](ADR-008.md). That record and the imported handoff remain historical sources. This direction does not assert an amended contract or a customer's acceptance of any particular implementation. Record adopted scope and decision authority in the [program adoption checklist](../../HOW-TO-USE.md#program-adoption-checklist).

## Rationale

Hardware and operational constraints can prevent universal implementation. A documented, reviewed limitation can be accepted without misrepresenting it as encrypted traffic or a passed technical test. Operational impact includes disrupted application/device behavior, performance and unnecessary user or administrative steps.

## Consequences

Maintain [implementation records](../../implementation/macsec.md) and a [customer acceptance register](../../acceptance/macsec.md), using the [record standard](../../standards/implementation-acceptance.md). The customer can accept a documented outcome with limitations; acceptance never changes a failed/unexecuted test into a pass. The decision record must identify its authority, date, rationale, scope, baseline and supporting artifact.

Do not silently downgrade a selected link's approved protection profile to preserve service. Stop or roll back the change under the approved process, record the result and request disposition. Numeric performance criteria for a selected implementation remain useful test criteria; they are not ZTA coverage percentages.

## Alternatives

A percentage of ports or controls is not an acceptance measure. Universal MACsec capability remains a historical aspiration, not a prerequisite for recording customer acceptance under the operationally bounded approach. Undocumented gaps and automatic acceptance based solely on closed stories are excluded.
