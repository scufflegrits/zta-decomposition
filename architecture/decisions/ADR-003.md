# ADR-003 — Preserve the Cisco/Palo Alto topology

**Decision ID:** ADR-003  
**Decision status:** Required  
**Source:** [Handoff section 5](../../zta-decomposition-handoff.md#5-established-architectural-decisions)

## Decision

At each site preserve Catalyst → ISR → Palo Alto → public WAN.

## Rationale

Retain the specified architecture baseline.

## Consequences

Place policy on actual paths; local ISR traffic may not traverse the Palo Alto.

## Alternatives and open selection

Reordering devices is outside the baseline; no earlier alternative analysis was supplied.

This record preserves the handoff baseline. Rationale and consequences are editorial explanations for review; they do not represent an additional approval or completed engineering test.
