# ADR-008 — MACsec coverage objective

**Decision ID:** ADR-008  
**Decision status:** Required objective / Verify  
**Source:** [Handoff section 5](../../zta-decomposition-handoff.md#5-established-architectural-decisions)

## Decision

Seek MACsec on every wired Ethernet port; resolve incompatibilities or approve explicit exceptions.

## Rationale

Protect compatible Ethernet links without hiding unsupported hardware.

## Consequences

Inventory exact ports and peers; consider alternate hardware/modules, architecture changes or approved compensating controls.

## Alternatives and open selection

Silent plaintext fallback and blanket capability assumptions are excluded.

This record preserves the handoff baseline. Rationale and consequences are editorial explanations for review; they do not represent an additional approval or completed engineering test.
